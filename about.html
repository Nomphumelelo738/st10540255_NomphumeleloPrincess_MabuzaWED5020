About Us

Get to know the story, purpose, and people behind the Crown of Hope Foundation.

Our History

The Crown of Hope Foundation was established in 2018 in Soweto, Johannesburg, with the mission of using community-based pageantry as a vehicle for social change. What began as a small local talent show has grown into an annual empowerment programme that supports over 200 young women annually through mentorship, scholarship funding, and leadership development.

The foundation partners with local schools, shelters, and corporate sponsors to create sustainable impact in underserved communities.

Our Mission

To empower young women through leadership development, educational scholarships, and community engagement, using the platform of pageantry to amplify voices and drive meaningful social change.

Our Vision

To become South Africa's leading youth empowerment foundation, creating a generation of confident, educated, and community-minded female leaders.
